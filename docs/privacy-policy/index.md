## 1. Introduction

Pinterest Organizer is a personal productivity application designed to help users analyze, categorize, and organize their saved Pinterest Pins and boards using artificial intelligence.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application accesses, how it is processed, and how users can control their data.

## 2. Information We Access

With the user's authorization, Pinterest Organizer may access:

- Pinterest account identifiers.
- Pinterest boards and board metadata.
- Saved Pins, including images, titles, descriptions, and links.
- Information necessary to organize and manage Pins.

The application accesses Pinterest information through the official Pinterest API using OAuth 2.0 authorization.

Pinterest Organizer does not request or store users' Pinterest passwords.

## 3. How We Use Information

The information is used exclusively to provide the application's functionality, including:

- Retrieving saved Pins and boards.
- Analyzing Pin images and descriptions.
- Identifying themes, objects, and categories.
- Suggesting improvements to board organization.
- Reorganizing Pins after explicit user approval.

Pinterest Organizer does not sell personal information or use Pinterest data for advertising.

## 4. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Pinterest Organizer may use third-party AI services, including OpenAI or Google Gemini, to analyze Pin images and descriptions.

Relevant Pin content may be transmitted to the selected AI provider for processing.

Such processing is performed to generate descriptions, classifications, and organizational recommendations.

The handling of information by these providers is governed by their applicable privacy policies and service agreements.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Pinterest Organizer is designed to operate primarily as a locally hosted application.

Pinterest data and generated classifications may be stored in a local database on the user's device.

OAuth access tokens are stored securely and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

The application does not operate a central database for collecting Pinterest users' information.

## 6. Data Sharing

Pinterest Organizer does not sell or share user data with advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be transmitted to selected AI service providers when necessary to perform requested analysis.

The application does not publicly disclose private Pinterest boards or saved Pins.

## 7. User Control

Users remain in control of their Pinterest content.

Pinterest Organizer provides:

- The ability to review suggested changes.
- Explicit approval before reorganizing Pins.
- The ability to disconnect Pinterest authorization.
- The ability to delete locally stored application data.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data is retained until the user deletes it or removes the application's local data storage.

Users may revoke the application's Pinterest access through their Pinterest account settings.

Revoking access prevents further authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously stored local data.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Pinterest Organizer interacts with the following third-party services:

- Pinterest — account authorization and content access.
- OpenAI and/or Google Gemini — optional AI-powered content analysis.

Each service maintains its own privacy policy.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data processing practices change.

The latest version will be available at this page.
